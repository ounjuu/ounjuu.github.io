Effective Date · 2026-05-13
"haetppo-together" (같이햇뽀, hereafter the "App") values your privacy and complies with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization, Apple App Store Guidelines, and the Google Play Console Data Safety policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Required at Sign-up

  • Nickname (2–12 characters, entered by the user)
  • 6-digit PIN (stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash by Firebase Authentication; plaintext is never stored anywhere)
※ Your nickname is hashed with SHA-1 and converted into a fake email under the externally-unreachable domain (@id.invalid, RFC 2606) used solely as a Firebase Auth identifier. It is not a real email address and is never sent or exposed externally.

B. Automatically Generated During Service Use

  • Sign-up timestamp, list of group (room) IDs you have joined
  • Room information: room name, subtitle, start date, category, invite code
  • Member information: nickname, selected character, level, joined-at timestamp, daily recurring items
  • Daily check-in checklist: item text, check state, attached photo URL

C. Automatically Collected Information

  • Advertising identifier (Android AAID / iOS IDFA) — for Google AdMob ad delivery
  • Device and OS information, app version — for ad optimization and crash debugging
  • Access logs — Firebase Authentication sign-in timestamps

D. Stored Only on Your Device (No Server Transmission)

  • Morning / evening notification ON·OFF toggle and notification times (stored locally on device only, never transmitted)
  • 4-digit app lock PIN (separate from the sign-up PIN; stored on device only)
  • Whether biometric authentication (Face ID / fingerprint) is enabled

E. OS Permissions Requested by the App

The App requests the following OS permissions. All permissions are optional; denying any of them only disables the related feature and does not prevent overall use of the App. You may change permissions at any time in your OS settings.

  • Camera (iOS · Android): used when taking a certification photo
  • Photo Library (iOS): used when selecting a certification photo from your gallery
  • Biometric / Face ID (iOS · Android USE_BIOMETRIC): used for the app lock feature
  • Notifications: used to deliver morning planning / evening check-in reminders. Notification content is generated locally on your device, and no user data is transmitted to an external server. You may decline at any time in OS settings; declining only disables notifications.
  • Internet access (Android INTERNET): for communication with external services such as Firebase, Cloudinary, and AdMob

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • User identification and sign-in
  • Providing group challenge / D-day check-in services
  • Granting milestone rewards
  • Displaying advertisements (revenue model)
  • Responding to abuse and abnormal activity

3. Retention and Use Period

  • Account information: until you withdraw (you can delete immediately via App settings → "Delete Account").
  • Groups and check-in records: kept until you leave the room as the last remaining member; once the last member leaves, all records are deleted.
  • Certification photos (Cloudinary): the App uses unsigned upload, so when you delete a checklist item the original file on Cloudinary is not immediately removed automatically by the App. Orphaned photos are cleaned up manually by the operator through the Cloudinary dashboard within the free hosting quota (25GB).
  • Advertising identifier: handled per Google AdMob policy (typically 13–18 months).

4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The App does NOT sell or provide your personal information to third parties. However, we entrust limited processing as described in Section 5 below, solely as necessary to provide the service.

To provide the service, the App transfers personal information internationally as described below. All communications are encrypted via HTTPS.

  • Recipient countries: United States, European Union (EU), Google global infrastructure (depending on the processor's data center)
  • Recipients: Google LLC (Firebase Authentication · Cloud Firestore · AdMob), Cloudinary, Inc.
  • Items transferred: information listed in Section 5 above
  • Purpose of transfer: providing the service as described in Section 2
  • Timing of transfer: transmitted as needed during sign-up and ongoing service use
  • Retention period: as described in Section 3

You may object to international transfer through the contact in Section 8 (in which case core features of the App may be limited).

7. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

  • Access: You can view your stored information directly in the App on the rooms, checklist, and reward screens.
  • Modification: Nicknames cannot be changed because they are used as the authentication key. Room name, character, checklist items, etc., can be freely modified in-app.
  • Deletion: You can immediately delete your account information, joined rooms, checklists, and photo metadata at once through App settings → "Delete Account".
  • Suspension: You can suspend processing immediately by signing out within the App.
  • These rights may also be exercised via the contact in Section 8.

8. Contact

  • Email: lunafortune.help@gmail.com

9. Security Measures

  • PINs are stored only as one-way bcrypt hashes by Firebase Authentication; even the operator cannot see the plaintext.
  • Nicknames are SHA-1 hashed and converted into externally-unreachable fake emails (@id.invalid) used only for authentication.
  • Firestore security rules block access to data outside the groups you belong to.
  • All external communications with Firebase, Cloudinary, etc., are encrypted via HTTPS.
  • Certification photos can only be uploaded by their owner, with restricted permissions.
  • Notifications are local notifications generated and displayed only on your device; notification content and settings are never transmitted externally.

10. How to Opt Out of Automated Data Collection

You can reset or opt out of tracking via your device's advertising settings.

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn OFF "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

The App serves ads through Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your OS ad settings; even after opting out, non-personalized ads may continue to be shown. Advertising IDs are collected by Google and processed per the Google Ads Policy.

11. Procedure and Method of Personal Information Disposal

  • When you tap "Delete Account" in the App, your Firebase Authentication account and Firestore data are deleted immediately.
  • Photos uploaded to Cloudinary are not automatically deleted from the App due to the unsigned upload model; the operator periodically cleans them up manually via the Cloudinary dashboard within the free hosting quota (25GB).
  • Electronic files are permanently deleted in a non-recoverable manner.

12. Personal Information of Children Under 14

Pursuant to Korean law, the App does NOT permit sign-up or use by children under the age of 14. Sign-up presumes that the user is 14 or older; accounts found to be under 14 will be deleted immediately.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • If this policy is updated, we will notify users in-app at least 7 days before the effective date.
  • For changes that materially affect user rights, we will provide notice at least 30 days in advance.
