Privacy Policy

LunaDiary puts your privacy first.
No sign-up. No cloud upload. No tracking.

1. Information We Collect

LunaDiary collects the following information to provide its services:

- Birth information: date of birth, time of birth, gender (entered by the user)
- Birthplace: place of birth (optional, for astrology services)

All information is stored only on the user's device (local storage) and is never transmitted to or stored on external servers.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored exclusively on the user's device and is never transmitted to any external server:

- Diary entries (mood, emotion tags, notes, entry date)
- Statistics data (monthly mood distribution, writing streaks, etc.)

3. Purpose of Collection

- To provide tarot, saju (Korean fortune telling), and astrology services
- To generate AI-powered fortune readings using birth information
- To provide diary writing and mood statistics features
- To enable sharing of fortune reading results as images

4. Diary Protection During AI Fortune Readings

When a fortune reading is requested, only the fortune category and the calculated zodiac/saju results are transmitted. Diary entries are never transmitted externally.

5. Data Deletion and Backup Policy

- Diary entries and user-entered information are permanently deleted when the app is deleted.
- No backup is stored separately, so recovery is not possible.
- There is no cloud synchronization feature.

6. Third-Party Sharing

LunaDiary does not share personal information with third parties.

However, birth information is sent to AI services (Google Gemini, Groq) solely for generating fortune readings. These services do not store the information. Diary entries are not sent to AI services.

7. Advertising

LunaDiary includes Google AdMob advertisements.

- Advertising identifiers may be used for ad delivery.
- Advertising identifiers are managed by the device and can be reset or disabled in device settings.
- No personally identifiable information is provided to advertising services.

8. User Rights

- Users can view, modify, or delete their birth information in the app settings at any time.
- All data can be completely removed by deleting the app.
- No account registration or login is required, so no withdrawal process is needed.

9. Security Measures

- Communication with AI services is encrypted via HTTPS.
- Personal data and diary entries are stored only on the device, minimizing the risk of external exposure.
- No account system means no risk of account compromise.

10. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be communicated through app updates.
