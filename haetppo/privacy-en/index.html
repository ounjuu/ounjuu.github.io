☀ Privacy Policy ☁

Effective Date · 2026-05-13
"Haetppo" (the "App") values your privacy and complies with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), Apple App Store Guidelines, and the Google Play Console Data Safety policy.

1. Information We Collect

Haetppo collects the following information to provide its services.

  • Location: GPS coordinates (used only while the App is in use, to look up local weather; optional). The App does NOT track your location in the background.
  • User input: selected character, body-type preference, gender, favorite cities, diary entries, and notification time settings
  • Advertising identifier: used for displaying AdMob ads (optional)

Location, character, body type, gender, cities, and diary entries are stored only on your device (Hive local storage) and are not transmitted to any external server.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Providing weather based on current location (calling public APIs of the Korea Meteorological Administration and Air Korea; only while the App is in use)
  • Personalized outfit recommendations (based on body type, gender, and temperature)
  • Diary writing and calendar accumulation
  • Sending local notifications (morning briefing, outfit recommendation, weather alerts, mission completion)
  • Displaying advertisements (Google AdMob)

3. Retention and Disposal

  • All personal information is stored only in your device's local storage (Hive).
  • All stored data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • You can delete stored information (cities, diary entries, mission progress, etc.) at any time within the App settings.
  • The App does not store personal information on any external server, so uninstalling the App fully erases your data.
  • Information displayed in the iOS Widget and Apple Watch app (current city, temperature, sky condition, air quality grade, selected character, outfit recommendation) is shared only on your device through an iOS App Group (group.com.haetppo.haetppo) and is not transmitted to any external server.
  • If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iOS device, the App's local data (cities, diary entries, etc.) may be automatically backed up to your own iCloud account. This is a feature of Apple iOS device backup, not a separate transmission by the App.

4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

Haetppo does NOT provide your personal information to third parties. However, the following external APIs receive limited non-identifying information solely to provide the service.

  • Korea Meteorological Administration short-term forecast API: latitude/longitude (for weather lookup, no personally identifying information)
  • Air Korea air pollution API: city/region name at province level (for air quality lookup, not personally identifying)
  • RainViewer (radar map): user location is NOT transmitted; only global radar tiles are downloaded
  • OpenStreetMap: map tile download (no personal information transmitted)

All communications above are encrypted via HTTPS.

5. Advertising

Haetppo includes advertisements served by Google AdMob.

  • An Advertising ID may be used to display ads.
  • The Advertising ID is managed automatically by the operating system and can be reset or disabled in your device settings.
  • No information that directly identifies you is provided to the advertising service.
  • iOS users can opt out of ad tracking through App Tracking Transparency.

The Google AdMob SDK may process limited information during ad display, including the advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID), IP address, device model, and OS version. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications

With your consent, Haetppo may send local notifications.

  • Daily morning weather briefing (at a user-specified time)
  • Daily outfit recommendation (at a user-specified time, with a separate toggle)
  • Weather alerts such as rain, snow, heatwaves, cold waves, and yellow dust
  • Mission completion notifications

Each notification type (morning briefing, outfit recommendation, weather alerts, mission completion) can be turned on or off individually in the App settings. All notifications are local notifications generated and displayed on your device only; the content and settings are not transmitted to any external server. You can revoke notification permission at any time from your OS settings or App settings.

7. User Rights

  • You can view, modify, and delete stored information (body type, gender, character, cities, theme, language, etc.) at any time from the App settings.
  • You can delete individual diary entries, and you can protect them by enabling Diary Lock (Face ID / fingerprint / PIN).
  • Mission progress and accumulated weather records are also removed when you uninstall the App.
  • You can completely remove all data by uninstalling the App.
  • The App has no sign-up or login, so simply uninstalling the App is sufficient — no separate withdrawal procedure is required.

8. Children Under 14

Haetppo does not separately collect personal information from children under 14 years of age.

9. Security Measures

  • All communications with external APIs (Korea Meteorological Administration, Air Korea, etc.) are encrypted via HTTPS.
  • Personal information is stored only in your device's local storage (Hive), minimizing the risk of external leakage.
  • The App has no account system, eliminating the risk of account takeover.
  • When you enable Diary Lock, your diary data is protected by biometric authentication (Face ID / fingerprint) or PIN.
  • The Diary Lock PIN is stored only in your device's Hive storage and is never transmitted externally. Biometric authentication (Face ID / fingerprint) is handled by the iOS / Android secure modules, and the App does not directly handle any biometric data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy is updated, we will notify users through an App update.
